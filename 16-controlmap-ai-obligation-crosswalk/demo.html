Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. Obligation statements are paraphrases written for this exercise, not the text of any rule. The generator decided which control meets which obligation, so the truth is known. No model is used. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (right-status rate with ControlMap − with the keyword sheet) × obligation entries tracked

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: the keyword sheet gives the right status to 23 of 48 obligations; ControlMap to 46 of 48. Pair precision is 97.4% and recall 96.2% against the truth map. ControlMap lists 10 of the 11 orphan controls and sends 20 evidence requests instead of 48. The dictionary was written by the author of the data, so these scores are an upper bound.

🟡 Modeled: 6 business lines each track their own copy of the 48-obligation list (an assumption, not a statistic), so 288 entries. (46/48 − 23/48) × 288 = 138 obligation entries given the right status. Assumes the copies behave like this set.
