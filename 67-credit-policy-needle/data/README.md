# Data for project 67

Synthetic. Generated by [`_generate.py`](./_generate.py) with a fixed seed, so a rerun reproduces every file byte for byte (`python3 _generate.py` from this folder). Nothing here comes from a real lender, customer, product or policy. The brand names are invented words, the clauses are built from word lists, and the limits are made up.

🟡 Assumptions, named: one synthetic page is 1,800 characters (a convention borrowed from [project 14](../../14-policyneedle-long-context-fidelity/data/README.md), not the layout of a real manual). Every limit and threshold, for example a maximum debt-to-income ratio between 34% and 46%, or a customer relationship of at least 12 to 36 months, is invented for the test. None is taken from a rule or a lender.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `manuals/manual_050p.txt`, `manual_150p.txt`, `manual_300p.txt` | The three documents an assistant is given |
| `manuals.csv` | One row per manual: characters, approximate pages, sections, clauses, planted exception blocks, look-alikes, and where the register starts |
| `probes.csv` | 210 questions with exact expected answers and where the evidence sits |
| `exceptions_truth.csv` | 141 rows: every planted exception clause (105) and every look-alike that is not one (36) |
| `example_manual.txt`, `example_probes.csv`, `example_answers.csv` | The 8-page example behind `python3 credit_policy_needle.py --demo` (8 questions, 4 rules) |

## The manuals

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `manual_050p` | 89,650 | 49.8 | 17 | 452 | 35 | 12 | 88,947 |
| `manual_150p` | 269,944 | 150.0 | 46 | 1,284 | 35 | 12 | 269,204 |
| `manual_300p` | 540,390 | 300.2 | 92 | 2,569 | 35 | 12 | 539,669 |

A manual is plain text. Blocks are separated by one blank line: two front-matter blocks, section headings (`Section 7. File Review: Card Services`), numbered clauses (`7.12 ...`) and, at the back, `Appendix A. Standing Exceptions Register` with entries numbered `R-1`, `R-2`. Most of each manual is filler: procedural clauses built from word lists, with no digits and none of the words a question or an exception uses (the generator checks this).

A rule is a limit in one clause: `The maximum loan term for Alder Personal Loan is 60 months.` There are five attributes (maximum debt-to-income ratio, minimum credit score, maximum loan term, maximum loan amount, maximum credit limit) on four product types (Personal Loan, Auto Loan, Credit Card, Business Line). Each rule has its own brand, so a product appears in one rule only. An exception loosens the limit for an applicant who meets one condition: a customer relationship, verified annual income, an initial payment or annual business revenue of at least some amount.

## The seven forms

Per manual and per depth (0, 25, 50, 75, 100 percent of the body, where the rule clause sits) there are two plain rules and one rule of each exception form.

| Form | Where the exception is planted | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| `plain` | No exception: the control. Two rules per depth | 1 each (the rule) |
| `inline` | In the rule clause: `..., except that it is 72 months for an applicant with ...` | applies, not met |
| `adjacent` | In the next clause, which names the product: `Exception to clause 23.6: ...` | applies, not met |
| `crossref` | In another section, citing the clause number only: `Notwithstanding clause 22.25, the limit stated there is ...` | applies, not met |
| `restated` | In another section, as a restated limit with no exception wording: `For an applicant with ..., the limit in clause 22.25 is ...` | applies, not met |
| `register` | An entry in Appendix A, at the back: `R-3 Register entry (effective 2026-01-31): clause 48.7 is ...` | applies, not met |
| `nested` | An adjacent exception, plus a far clause: `Clause 23.7 does not apply to an applicant with a payment default recorded in the prior 12 months.` | applies, carved out |

The far clause of a `crossref`, `restated` or `nested` rule sits at a fixed other depth (rule at 0 percent, far clause at 50; 25 and 75; 50 and 0; 75 and 25; 100 and 50). The register entry always sits at the back.

**Look-alikes** (12 per manual, 4 of each): `decoy_prohibition` (`No exception to clause X is permitted without the written approval of the credit committee.`), `decoy_generic` (`Except for the cover sheet, each sheet of the file shows the case number in the margin.`) and `decoy_pointer` (`Reviewers cite clause X in the case log, except for files closed with no decision.`). They are there to see what the audit does with text that uses exception words and changes no limit.

## `probes.csv`: 210 questions

70 per manual: 30 `plain`, 90 `applies` (the applicant meets the condition, so the exception value is the answer), 75 `not_met` (the applicant is below the threshold, so the rule value is the answer) and 15 `carved_out` (the applicant meets the condition but had a payment default recorded in the last 12 months, so the rule value is the answer).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `probe_id`, `manual_id`, `length_pages`, `doc_chars` | For example `P150-NST-050-1`: 150-page manual, form NST (nested), depth 50, question 1 |
| `form`, `depth_pct`, `variant` | The form, the depth label, and which of the questions above it is |
| `question`, `expected_answer`, `answer_kind` | One exact answer: a percent, points, months or dollars |
| `trap_answer` | The wrong answer a reader gives when it misses the exception (or applies one that does not hold) |
| `base_id`, `exception_id`, `carve_id` | Clause numbers of the rule, the exception, and the cancelling clause |
| `base_pct`, `exception_pct` | Where the rule and the exception start, as a percent of the document |
| `base_end`, `exception_end`, `carve_end` | Character offset where each block ends. A reader that sees only the first N characters sees a block only if its end is at most N |

## `exceptions_truth.csv`

`manual_id`, `block_id`, `kind`, `is_exception`, `cites`. The kind is `inline`, `adjacent`, `crossref`, `restated`, `register`, `nested_exception` or `carve_out` for the 105 exception blocks, and `decoy_prohibition`, `decoy_generic` or `decoy_pointer` for the 36 look-alikes. The generator writes this table while it plants the text; the audit in `src/credit_policy_needle.py` never sees it.

## The example set

`example_manual.txt` is 8 pages with four rules (inline at 25 percent, adjacent at 50, crossref at 75, register at 50) and one look-alike of each kind. `example_answers.csv` holds what an assistant that searches on the rule's own words would say: the rule value wherever the exception sits away from the rule. I wrote those answers by hand in the generator. They are not produced by the readers in `src/common.py`.

## How the files connect

```
_generate.py --+--> manuals/*.txt         the documents a reader is given
               +--> manuals.csv
               +--> probes.csv ---------> src/common.py readers --> src/credit_policy_needle.py score and gate
               +--> exceptions_truth.csv -> src/step_04_with_gate.py scores the audit
               +--> example_*              src/credit_policy_needle.py --demo
```

Reproducibility: every 🟢 number in the README is printed by a script in `src/` that reads these files. Regenerate, rerun, and the output matches to the digit.
