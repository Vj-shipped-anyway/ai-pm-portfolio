Credit Policy Needle: where a reader loses the exception

Synthetic data. Three invented credit policy manuals (50, 150 and 300 pages) and 210 questions. The four readers are small functions, not models: a control and three with one planted fault each. Each cell shows the questions answered correctly out of the questions asked, for one form of exception at one depth of the rule clause.

The audit, scored against the planted exceptions

Known misses: the audit does not list an exception worded as a restated limit, and it lists a pointer that says "except" though it is not an exception. A reader that never opens an exception passes every question where the exception does not apply; the paired question where it does apply is what fails it. Source: python3 step_04_with_gate.py.
