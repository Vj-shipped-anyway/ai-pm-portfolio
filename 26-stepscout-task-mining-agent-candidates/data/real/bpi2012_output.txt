1. What the log holds
   13,087 cases, 262,200 events, 24 activities, 68 resources
   6 of the activities are work items (W_), with 71,371 START-to-COMPLETE sessions (1,042 completes without a START)

2. Paths: how many real variants, and how much the most common one covers
   a variant is the ordered list of COMPLETE events of a case (A_, O_ and W_ activities)
   4,336 distinct variants over 13,087 cases
   the most common variant covers 3,429 cases (26.2%)
   1,719 variants are needed to cover 80% of cases
   3,727 variants occur once (28.5% of cases)

3. Hands-on time against elapsed time
   elapsed, first to last event per case:        2,708,646 h
   hands-on, START to COMPLETE of work sessions: 14,357 h (0.53% of elapsed)
   waiting, SCHEDULE to first START of a task:   738,395 h (98.1% of waiting plus hands-on)

4. Rework and resumption
   26,318 tasks were scheduled; 2,311 cases (17.7%) schedule the same activity again (4,165 repeat tasks)
   45,133 sessions resume a task already started; they hold 7,280 hands-on hours

6. What this log cannot show
   - No desktop layer: work in a spreadsheet, an email or a second application is not observable, so
     the off-system share that StepScout measures on the synthetic data cannot be measured here.
   - No control register and no answer key: no step is classified and no accuracy is claimed.
   - START to COMPLETE is the time a work item was open, not proof of hands on the keyboard.
   - One institution, one process, 2011-2012. The numbers describe this log and are not a benchmark.
