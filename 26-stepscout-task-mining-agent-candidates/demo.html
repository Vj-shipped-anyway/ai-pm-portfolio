Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. Steps run in workflow order and the totals add touch hours from S01 to the selected step. The answer key is the generator's own, and the classifier never reads it. Hours at stake are modeled: an agent removes 70% of a step's touch time, an assumption. Task mining of employee desktops is a privacy and works-council question this prototype only designs for. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (agent-ready hours at stake per worker in StepScout's top 10 − in the volume-first top 10) × workers

🟢 Measured on the answer key: the volume-first top 10 holds 6 agent-ready steps and 4 that touch restricted data or a four-eyes control, with no control screen. StepScout's top 10 holds 9 agent-ready steps. It classifies 13 of 16 steps correctly (81.2%), with 92.3% precision and 100.0% recall for agent-ready steps. Known miss: S14 choose_resolution_route, ranked #10.

🟡 Modeled: (42.3 − 26.5) = 15.8 hours per worker per 30 days × 14 workers = 221 hours per 30 days, assuming an agent removes 70% of touch time and both teams build their top 10.
