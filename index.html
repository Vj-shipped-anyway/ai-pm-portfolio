How AI gets into bank operations

I'm curious how AI actually gets into bank operations, and what breaks when it does. These are seventy-seven runnable prototypes on synthetic data: twenty-seven case studies and fifty smaller checks you can run. Each one names how the common approach fails, builds the layer that contains it, and prints what it still gets wrong. I'm open to AI delivery and product leadership in bank operations, and to AI founders selling into banks.

I price product impact with one formula:
Utility = (my solution − current state of the art) × number of people it affects

These are portfolio prototypes on synthetic data, not production case studies. Every number is tagged measured, modeled or hypothetical; see how to read the numbers. LeaseGuard and DealSentry apply the same approach to commercial real estate, a domain I follow as a personal study interest.

Start here

If you run AI in a bank's operations

  • StepScout Which steps can an agent take over? A volume-first top 10 holds 6 agent-ready steps; StepScout's holds 9.
  • TierGate Which use cases get in? The tier matches the reference on 129 of 150 submissions, against 104 self-declared.
  • AgentKit How does an engineer pick up the build? 522 of 548 shadow-mode proposals agree with the human outcome.
  • ScopeKeeper What can the agent touch? 61 of 71 unneeded grants removed; the annual review removes 1.
  • KillSwitch What happens when it degrades? Trips on 4 of 5 held-out events; 0 of 7 drill scenarios are ready.

If you build AI for banks

  • VendorLens Can we trust this vendor? A questionnaire passes 29 of 40, including 6 of the 10 truly high-risk vendors.
  • TierGate Which tier and evidence does it need? Approvals missing evidence: 32 of 116 self-declared, 3 of 76 checked.
  • ControlMap Which obligations map to which controls? Right status on 46 of 48, against 23 of 48 for a keyword sheet.
  • CorpusGate Who can retrieve what? 291 of 1,000 results leak without a check, 11 with it.
  • ScopeKeeper Is the agent over-permissioned? 61 of 71 unneeded grants removed; the annual review removes 1.

All numbers above are measured on synthetic data. What surprised me: ten results that changed how I read the common approach. How I would run it: an operating model for AI in bank operations, built from the twenty-seven (a design, not a deployment).
