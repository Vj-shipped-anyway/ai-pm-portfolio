Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. Totals run from E01 to the selected event and count expected bad decisions inside event windows. The trip level was fitted on days 1-60 and scored on days 61-90. The rules path quality numbers are stubs set by the generator, and no model is used anywhere. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (bad decisions per event without KillSwitch − with it) × degradation events per quarter

🟢 Measured on the 5 held-out events (days 61-90): 255 bad decisions per event with the tuned single threshold, 223 with KillSwitch, so 32 avoided per event. KillSwitch tripped for 4 of 5 events, median 2.5 hours in, with 1 false trip. The single threshold tripped for 1.

🟡 Modeled: 4 degradation events a quarter (an assumption, not a statistic) gives 32 × 4 = 128 bad decisions avoided per quarter against the single threshold. Against the manual plan the figure is 75 per event, 300 per quarter, and rests on a 4 hour decision delay and a complaint-cluster notice level of 12 that are assumptions.
