Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The generator decided each incident's category, severity and root cause, and who sent which notice when, so the truth is known. No model is used. Deadline lengths and rubric thresholds are assumptions of a modeled bank, not legal advice. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/; the clocks are read at 2026-10-05 00:00.

Utility delivered

Utility = (deadline misses and unstarted clocks per incident, queue − IncidentLedger) × incidents per quarter

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: of 87 notification deadlines, 39 were missed (17 sent late, 22 never sent) and 8 never started. The queue showed none of them: 39 + 8 = 47 problems over 120 incidents (0.392 per incident). IncidentLedger flags all 39 missed clocks and leaves the 8 that cannot start without a determination (0.067 per incident). Category right on 110 of 120; the 10 misses are the hard cases written into the data.

🟡 Modeled: 60 AI incidents a quarter (an assumption, not a statistic; the synthetic set holds 120) gives (0.392 − 0.067) × 60 = about 19.5 missed deadlines avoided a quarter. Assumes a person acts on every countdown before the deadline.
