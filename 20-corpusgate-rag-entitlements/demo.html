Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The generator decided every access list, so the truth is known. The retriever is a stub (plain TF-IDF), not a model; no model is used. The totals above cover all 200 test questions and every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (unreadable results per 100 queries without CorpusGate − with it) × assistant users

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: 291 of 1,000 results went to a user who may not read them (145.50 per 100 queries), and the relevance eval scored 283 of them as correct. With CorpusGate 11 remain (5.50 per 100 queries), all from the 3 ACL changes made after the last sync. The cheap guards alone leave 47 (ACL copy from index time), 52 (flat group check) and 114 (label-only filter). Readable recall@5 goes from 0.707 to 0.812; holding personal-data documents out of the index costs 0.070 of that.

🟡 Modeled: 1,200 assistant users (an assumption, not a statistic) gives (145.50 − 5.50) × 1,200 = 168,000 unreadable results kept from users if each runs 100 queries.
