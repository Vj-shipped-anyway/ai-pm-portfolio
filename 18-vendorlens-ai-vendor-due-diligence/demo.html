Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. Vendor ids and model providers are invented codes, and the generator decided every vendor's true risk. No model is used: the scoring is plain rules. Weights, the HIGH line and the 40% concentration line are my assumptions. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (high-risk vendors found per vendor with VendorLens − with the questionnaire) × vendors in a bank's AI estate

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: the questionnaire passes 29 of 40 vendors, including 6 of the 10 that are truly high risk. A failed questionnaire finds 4 of 10 (4 of 11 fails, precision 36.4%). VendorLens rates 8 vendors HIGH and 5 are truly high risk (precision 62.5%, recall 50.0%). At the same queue size of 11, 6 of 10 are in VendorLens's top 11. MP-1 carries 42.9% of critical use cases; the truth is 53.6% because 5 vendors do not disclose their provider.

🟡 Modeled: 120 vendors in a bank's AI estate (an assumption, not a statistic) gives (0.125 − 0.100) × 120 = 3.0 more high-risk vendors found. 40 vendors is a small test set: one vendor moves a rate by 2.5 points.
