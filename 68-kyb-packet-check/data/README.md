# Data for project 68

Synthetic. Generated by `_generate.py` with a fixed seed, so a rerun reproduces every file byte for byte. Names are invented combinations; any match with a real person or business is a coincidence. Dates of birth and addresses are random, and an ID number is only its last four digits. Nothing here comes from a real filing, bank or employer.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `packets.csv` | 75 packets: `packet_id`, `source_text` (the text of the filing the model was shown) |
| `extractions.csv` | what a model returned for each packet, as JSON: `packet_id`, `extraction_json`. Recorded, not live: the extraction is a stub that replays this file |
| `truth.csv` | the planted truth: `scenario`, `defect` (1 when a misread was planted), `true_beneficial_owners`, `true_control_person`, `planted_detail`. The label comes from how the packet was built, not from the checks |
| `example_source.txt`, `example_extracted.json` | the worked example behind `python3 kyb_packet_check.py --demo` (two holders' percents swapped) |

## Packet text
Labeled lines in one of three holder-row formats and one of two person-line formats, chosen per packet. A line that starts with `Look-through:` gives an owner's share of an entity holder. Percentages always carry a `%`. Dates are ISO (YYYY-MM-DD). The control person, the certification and an optional note are lines of their own.

## Extraction JSON
`holders` (name, kind `individual` or `entity`, pct of the customer), `lookthrough` (entity, name, pct of that entity), `people` (name, address, dob, id_last4), `control_person` (name, title), `certification` (signed_by, date), `legal_name`, `packet_id`. Only `holders` is required; a missing or empty value is a completeness finding, not a crash.

## Scenarios (75)
Clean, 32: direct individuals 12 (four of them sit on or just under the 25 percent line, at 25 and 24), one entity holder with look-through 8 (including an individual who reaches the threshold only by adding a direct and an indirect share, and one at 24 behind an entity), thirds and sixths written to two decimals 4 (the schedule adds to 99.99 or 100.02), names written in another form 5, an entity holding 20 percent with no look-through shown 3.

Planted misreads, 43: percent off by a factor of ten 5, owner left out 5, percents swapped between two holders 6 (4 across the 25 line, 2 on the same side), entity listed with no owners behind it 4, control person not read 4, a detail left blank 5, a surname or the legal name misspelled 4, month and day of a birth date swapped 4, certification signature or date not read 3, a nominee note in the filing that the extraction ignores 3. The last kind is hard on purpose: the extraction matches the schedule, and no rule in `src/` reads the note.

🟡 Assumptions in the checks, not taken from any rule: a schedule is complete when it adds to 100, and the allowed distance from 100 is 0.1 percentage point.
