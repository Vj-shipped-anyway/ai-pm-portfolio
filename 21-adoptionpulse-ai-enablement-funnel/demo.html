Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The generator decided what each person does, so the truth is known. No model is used: the stage rules are plain thresholds (assumptions of a modeled bank). The champion and training effects are planted by construction. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (share of licensed users correctly staged, with AdoptionPulse − with the login flag) × licensed users

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: AdoptionPulse stages 762 of 800 users (95.2%) the way the generator decided. The login flag, mapped to the four stages with hindsight so it gets its best case, gets 407 of 800 (50.9%). 337 of 558 activated users never became habitual, and the login view counts all of them as adopted. Known limit: 13 of 80 users staged proficient produce poor work, which weekly counts cannot show.

🟡 Modeled: (95.2% − 50.9%) × 800 licensed staff = about 355 users correctly staged. The licensed population is an assumption (the modeled bank's full license count), and it assumes the accuracy measured here holds on real usage data.
