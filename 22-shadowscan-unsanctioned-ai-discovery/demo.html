Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. 24 picks: the highest-exposure domains found, four edge cases, then the ten departments. The generator decided which domains are AI tools, so the truth is known. No model is used: the classifier is a catalog lookup, a feed lookup, a path pattern and a byte-ratio test. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (sessions found per staff member with ShadowScan − with a blocklist) × staff using AI outside the catalog

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: the blocklist finds 2,275 of 4,200 unsanctioned AI sessions (11.849 per staff member who used one); ShadowScan finds 3,948 (20.562 per staff member), 1,673 of them beyond the list. It finds 28 of 30 unapproved AI domains; the list hits 14. Precision is 87.9% against 66.4% for the list.

🟡 Modeled: 10,000 staff × 15% using AI outside the catalog = 1,500 people (an assumption, not a statistic) gives (20.562 − 11.849) × 1,500 = about 13,070 extra sessions brought into view per two-week window. The 600 off-network sessions stay invisible to both.
