Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The generator decided which evidence is missing, stale or false, so the truth is known. No model is used: status, capacity, dependency and pause rules are plain rules. Benefit figures are owner estimates, never measured. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (programs shown with the true status: PortfolioBoard − report) ÷ programs × people affected

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: 23 of 30 programs change status once evidence is applied. The report shows the true status for 7 of 30 programs; PortfolioBoard for 27 of 30. 4 of 19 programs reported green fail a gate criterion, model risk review is short in 4 of 4 quarters, and 3 false attestations stay invisible to the board.

🟡 Modeled: 25 distinct owners plus 10 sponsors (an assumption of one sponsor per 3 programs) gives 35 people. (27 − 7) ÷ 30 × 35 = 23.3 owners and sponsors who see a true status for their program. Assumes the board's statuses are read and acted on.
