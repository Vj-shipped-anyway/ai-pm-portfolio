Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The generator decided which grants each agent needs, so the truth is known. No model is used: the diff, proposal, replay and ranking are plain rules. Every number on this page comes from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (unneeded grants removed per agent with ScopeKeeper − with annual recertification) × agents in the estate

🟢 Measured on this synthetic set: 71 of 184 grants are not needed by the agent that holds them. The annual review removed 1 of them (1.4%); ScopeKeeper removed 61 (85.9%). That is 4.07 against 0.07 unneeded grants removed per agent. The dry-run replay of the 90 days denied 0 of 7,994 calls (by construction). The known limit: 8 quarterly grants are removed too, and the next quarter would deny 51 legitimate calls until owners mark them periodic.

🟡 Modeled: 200 agents in a bank's estate (an assumption, not a statistic) gives (4.07 − 0.07) × 200 = about 800 unneeded grants removed, and about 680 legitimate calls denied next quarter until the periodic scopes are attested.
