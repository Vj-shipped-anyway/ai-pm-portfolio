Portfolio prototype on synthetic data, not a production case study. The stats above cover all 600 cases; the three cards run the selected case through manual handling (the recorded outcome), the step 2 script and the kit in shadow mode (every write a dry run). The reasoning step is a STUB: a deterministic template, not a language model. The recorded human outcome is the generator's idea of a careful worker, not ground truth about any real operation. Every number on this page is generated from the scripts in src/.

Utility delivered

Utility = (my solution − current state of the art) × number of people it affects

🟢 Measured on the 600 historical cases: the script gets 127 cases wrong or unsafe in at least one way (21.2%). AgentKit finishes 134 cases on its own and 15 of those differ from the recorded human outcome (2.5% of all cases). 8 of 9 acceptance criteria pass; AC-06 (auto-handled wrongly, limit 1%) fails at 11.2%, so under the brief's own exit criteria the kit stays in shadow mode.

🟡 Modeled: 1,800 cases a quarter (an assumption: 600 cases in 30 days, three such windows, flat volume) gives a lift of 18.7% × 1,800 = 336 fewer wrong or unsafe outcomes taken without a person per quarter.

🔴 Not counted: 6 wrong proposals still reach a person at S09. A real reviewer's catch rate is not measured. The approver in the enforcement run was a scripted test double.
